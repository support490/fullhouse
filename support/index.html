Frequently asked questions

I got a new phone — does my progress come with me?
Full House has no accounts and no cloud save — all progress is stored locally on your device, so it does not transfer to a new phone. What you can bring over are one-time purchases (the Season Pass, Midnight Deck, and Velvet Table): on the new device, open the More tab → Restore purchases using the same Apple/Google account you bought them with. Gems and game progress stay on the original device.
How do I restore my purchases?
Open the More tab → Restore purchases in the app while using the same Apple/Google account you bought them on. This restores one-time purchases (the Season Pass and cosmetic items). Consumable Gem packs are delivered to the device they were bought on and cannot be restored or transferred.
Can I win real money?
No. Full House is a single-player game played entirely with virtual currency. Cash, Gold, Gems, and Crowns have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out or exchanged for money. There is no real-money gambling.
How do I get a refund for a purchase?
In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them:
  • iOS / App Store: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android / Google Play: visit your Google Play account and request a refund.
If you were charged in error, email us and we'll help.
How do I delete my data?
There is no account to delete — Full House has no accounts and we collect no data. To wipe your local game data, open the More tab → Reset save in the app (this is permanent), or simply delete the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What are the Vault, Crowns, and Regulars?
The Vault is your compounding meta-progression: every run banks Gold that powers a permanent global multiplier and earns interest while you're away. Crowns are earned by ascending and unlock permanent prestige upgrades. Regulars are the quirky characters you hire during a run to stack chips and multipliers.
How does the Arena work? Am I playing real people?
No — the Arena is local and offline. You climb a seeded ladder of 12 named bot rivals; there are no real opponents and no online leaderboard. Seasons last 3 days and settle on your device, paying out in-game rewards (Gold, Crowns, or Gems) at season end.
How do hints and paused runs work?
Your first hint each round is free; extra hints cost 10 Gems. Resuming a paused run is always free.
The game crashed or something looks wrong.
Sorry about that! Email us with your device model, OS version, and app version, plus a screenshot if you have one, and we'll look into it.
