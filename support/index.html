Frequently asked questions

I got a new phone — does my progress come with me?
Full House has no accounts and no cloud save of ours. Your progress is stored on your device, so it moves to a new phone only if your device's own backup or transfer carries it (iCloud backup or Quick Start on iPhone, Google backup or the transfer tool on Android). One-time purchases — the Founder's Pass, Starter Pack, Midnight Deck and Velvet Table — always come back: open More → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple or Google account.
How do I restore my purchases?
Open More → Restore purchases while using the Apple or Google account you bought them with. This restores one-time purchases (the Founder's Pass, Starter Pack and cosmetic items); the Gems bundled with the Pass and Starter Pack are delivered once per purchase, including after a reset. Gem packs are consumable and can't be restored.
Is this gambling? Can I win real money?
No and no. Full House is a single-player card game for all players: nothing is ever wagered, and Cash, Gold, Gems, Crowns and Trophies have no real-world value and can't be cashed out. The pace choice before a round only sets a bigger goal for a bigger in-game reward — nothing is risked. Nothing sold in the game is random.
What do purchases do?
Purchases are optional. Cosmetics change only how the game looks. Gems — bought or earned by playing — also buy a few helpers: Second Wind (one more hand after a failed round, 25 Gems), hints (10 Gems after the free one each round), streak shields (50 Gems) and Vault Boost (+50% Vault earnings for 24 hours, once every 3 days, 80 Gems). Tables, Regulars, Decks, Crowns and levels can't be bought.
How do reminders work?
Reminders are off until you turn them on in More → Reminders. Once on, the app schedules at most two local notifications a day, only between 9am and 9pm: a new Daily Deal, a streak at risk, an Arena season ending, and a full Vault. Switch any of them off in the More tab or in your device settings. Nothing is sent to us.
How do I get a refund for a purchase?
In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them: on iPhone or iPad visit reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, open your Google Play account and request a refund. If you were charged in error, email us and we'll help.
How do I delete my data?
There is no account to delete — Full House has no accounts and we collect no data. To wipe your local game data, open More → Reset save (this is permanent), or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.
What are the Vault, Tables, Crowns and Regulars?
The Vault is your lasting progress: every run banks Gold that powers a permanent global multiplier, and between visits the Vault fills over 8 hours with a share of what your runs make — at most one full Vault a day. Tables are difficulty tiers: clear Round 10 on a Table to unlock the next, with harder goals and more Gold. Crowns come from Ascending and from Arena seasons. Regulars are the 40 friendly helpers you hire during a run to bend the math.
How does the Arena work? Am I playing real people?
No — the Arena is local and offline. You climb a seeded ladder of 12 named bot rivals; there are no real opponents and no online leaderboard. Seasons last 3 days and settle on your device, paying in-game rewards (Gold, Gems, Trophies or Crowns).
Why does the Daily Deal say I already played today?
The Daily Deal is one try per calendar day on your device, and everyone gets the same cards and rule. A fresh deal arrives at midnight local time. Your streak counts consecutive days; one free shield each week covers a missed day.
