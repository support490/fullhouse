Frequently asked questions

I got a new phone — does my progress come with me?
Full House has no accounts and no cloud save — all progress is stored locally on your device, so it does not transfer to a new phone. What you can bring over are one-time purchases (the Founder's Pass, Starter Pack, Midnight Deck, and Velvet Table): on the new device, open the More tab → Restore purchases using the same Apple/Google account you bought them with. Gems and game progress stay on the original device.
How do I restore my purchases?
Open the More tab → Restore purchases in the app while using the same Apple/Google account you bought them on. This restores one-time purchases (the Founder's Pass, Starter Pack and cosmetic items); the Gems bundled with the Pass and Starter Pack are delivered once per purchase, including on a restore after a reset. Consumable Gem packs are delivered to the device they were bought on and cannot be restored or transferred.
Is this gambling? Can I win real money?
No and no. Full House is a single-player card game for everyone: nothing is ever wagered, and Cash, Gold, Gems, Crowns and Trophies have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out. The "pace" choice before a round only sets a bigger goal for a bigger in-game reward — nothing is risked.
How do I get a refund for a purchase?
In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them:
  • iOS / App Store: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android / Google Play: visit your Google Play account and request a refund.
If you were charged in error, email us and we'll help.
How do I delete my data?
There is no account to delete — Full House has no accounts and we collect no data. To wipe your local game data, open the More tab → Reset save in the app (this is permanent), or simply delete the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What are the Vault, Tables, Crowns and Regulars?
The Vault is your meta-progression: every run banks Gold that powers a permanent global multiplier, and the Vault keeps earning a modest share of what your runs make for up to 8 hours while you're away (never more than half a run's worth per visit). Tables are difficulty tiers — clear Round 10 on your current Table to unlock the next, with bigger goals, new rules and more Gold. Crowns come from Ascending (resetting your Gold for a permanent boost) and from winning Arena seasons. Regulars are the quirky helpers you hire during a run; new ones are revealed as you level up and invited into your shop pool with Gold.
How does the Arena work? Am I playing real people?
No — the Arena is local and offline. You climb a seeded ladder of 12 named bot rivals; there are no real opponents and no online leaderboard. Seasons last 3 days and settle on your device, paying out in-game rewards (Gold, Crowns, or Gems) at season end.
Why did the Daily Deal say I already played today?
The Daily Deal is one try per calendar day on your device. A fresh deal arrives at midnight local time. Your streak counts consecutive days; one free shield per week covers a missed day.
How do hints and paused runs work?
Your first hint each round is free; extra hints cost 10 Gems. Resuming a paused run is always free.
The game crashed or something looks wrong.
Sorry about that! Email us with your device model, OS version, and app version, plus a screenshot if you have one, and we'll look into it.
