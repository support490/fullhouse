8. Arena seasons & rewards

The Arena is a local, offline feature: you climb a seeded ladder of 12 named bot rivals whose scores track your own recent form. It is not live multiplayer, there is no online leaderboard, and you never play against real people. Seasons run for 3 days and settle on your device with in-game rewards (Gold, Gems, Trophies or Crowns) — all Virtual Items with no real-world value. We may change, rebalance, or end these features in updates.
