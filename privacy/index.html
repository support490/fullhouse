Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: we do not collect any of your data. Full House has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. Who we are

The Full House app is operated by Finlock Accounting LLC, doing business as Pythn Development. Questions: support@pythn.app

2. Information we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, store, or transmit any personal information. The app makes no network calls of its own; the only external communication is in-app purchase processing by the Apple App Store or Google Play, which happens directly between your device and Apple or Google.

  • Game progress (Vault balance, Gold, Gems, Crowns, levels, quests, streaks, Compendium, Arena results, cosmetics, settings) is stored on your device. It is never sent to us. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it, under your device settings and Apple's or Google's policies.
  • Purchase history is held by Apple or Google as part of your App Store / Google Play account, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details.

3. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

We do not track you. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or diagnostics SDKs, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. If that ever changes in a future version, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where required.

4. Reminders

Reminders are off until you turn them on. If you do, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for example, when a new Daily Deal is ready) — at most two a day, between 9am and 9pm. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing leaves your device. You can turn each reminder off in the More tab or in your device settings.

5. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud save of ours

Full House has no user accounts and no sign-in of any kind. All progress is saved on your device; there is no cloud save of ours and no sync between devices. Your device's own backup or transfer tools may carry the save to a new device; without them, deleting the app deletes your progress. One-time purchases (the Founder's Pass, Starter Pack and cosmetic items) are tied to your Apple or Google account and can always be restored via Restore purchases in the More tab.

6. In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, governed by their privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item on your device.

7. Deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side — and there is no account to delete, because there are no accounts. To erase your local game data: open the More tab → Reset save (permanently wipes all local game data), or simply delete the app from your device. To manage purchase records held by Apple or Google, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

8. Children

Full House is for all players. It has no chat, no accounts, no ads and no data collection, so it collects no personal information from anyone — including children.

9. Security

Your game data is never sent to us, which removes most transmission and server-side risks. The data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption, OS protections), so keep your device secured and up to date.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if a future version adds features that change how data is handled. We will post the new version with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

Full House is operated by Finlock Accounting LLC (d/b/a Pythn Development). Questions: support@pythn.app
