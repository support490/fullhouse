Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Full House ("Full House", "we", "us", or "our") is a single-player cozy poker roguelike. The short version of this policy: we do not collect any of your data. Full House has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. Who we are

The Full House app and this website are operated by Finlock Accounting LLC, doing business as Pythn Development. If you have any questions, contact us at support@pythn.app.

2. Information we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, store, or transmit any personal information. The app makes no network calls of its own. The only external communication from the app is in-app purchase processing by the Apple App Store or Google Play, which happens directly between your device and Apple or Google.

We do not track you, and the app contains no advertising, no analytics or diagnostics SDKs, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. If that ever changes in a future version, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where required.

3. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync

  • Full House has no user accounts and no sign-in of any kind.
  • All progress is saved locally on your device. There is no cloud save and progress does not sync or transfer between devices.
  • If you delete the app, your local progress is deleted with it. The exception is non-consumable purchases (the Season Pass and cosmetic items), which are tied to your Apple or Google account and can be restored via Restore purchases in the app's More tab.
  • Consumable Gem purchases are delivered to the device they were bought on and are not restorable or transferable.

4. In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Their handling of your payment and account data is governed by their own privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item on your device.

5. Deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side — and there is no account to delete, because there are no accounts. To erase your local game data:

  • In the app: open the More tab → Reset save. This permanently wipes all local game data.
  • Or simply delete the app from your device, which removes its local data.

To manage or delete purchase records held by Apple or Google, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

6. Children's privacy

Full House is not directed to children. The game contains simulated gambling (poker with virtual currency only) and is rated for ages 17 and up. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

7. Security

Your game data never leaves your device, which removes most transmission and server-side risks. The data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption, and OS protections), so we recommend keeping your device secured and up to date.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if a future version of the game adds features that change how data is handled. We will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated in the app where appropriate.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email support@pythn.app.
